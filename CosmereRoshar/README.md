![About](../.github/assets/roshar/about.png)

*Roshar, and what it takes to live there.*

- **Surgebinding**
    - Ten Surges, split across ten Radiant Orders. Every Order gets two of them and a gemstone
    - Lashings, Soulcasting, Regrowth, Illumination, Elsecall, and the rest of the Surge abilities
    - Stormlight fuels all of it, and it drains fast

- **The Nahel Bond**
    - Catch a spren, hold it, bond it
    - Five Ideals per Order. Each one you speak raises how much Stormlight you can hold and opens new abilities
    - Act against your oaths and the bond strains, then fractures, then breaks

- **Shardblades and Shardplate**
    - Radiant Blades, and dead Blades that take ten heartbeats to summon
    - Shardplate and Shardhelms that run on Stormlight

- **Stormlight and Gems**
    - Chips, marks, and broams, infused and spent
    - Sphere pouches, and gemhearts you can split
    - Fabrials: augmenters, diminishers, painrials, and Stormlight power generation

- **Highstorms and the Weeping**
    - Highstorms cross the map on a schedule. Build real shelter or lose pawns
    - Everything left outside recharges. Everything left outside also dies
    - The Weeping stops the storms, and the Stormlight with them

- **Rosharan Society**
    - Lighteyes and darkeyes, ranked by nahn and dahn
    - Alethi warcamps, listener tribes, Thaylen merchants, and Shin farmers

- **The Nightwatcher**
    - Ask for a boon. Take the curse that comes with it

- **Scenarios**
    - Bridge Four, the Shattered Plains, Words of Radiance, the Fall of Kholinar, the siege of Urithiru,
      the Weeping, and the True Desolation

​

![Compatibility](../.github/assets/roshar/compatibility.png)

- Requires Biotech
- Works alongside Ideology, Royalty, and Anomaly
- Built for RimWorld 1.6
- Safe to add at game start
- Mid-save support is partial. Start a new colony if you want the full spread of gems and resources

​

![Tips](../.github/assets/roshar/tips.png)

- Build roofs and real shelter early. Highstorms are not scenery
- Keep infused spheres or another Stormlight source on hand before sending Radiants into a fight
- Treat the Nahel bond with care. Broken oaths should cost something
- Start a fresh colony for the full Roshar experience while these systems are still in beta

![Recommendations](../.github/assets/roshar/recommendations.png)

Mods that pair well with the Cosmere mods:

* [Nice Bill Tab](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3520130671)
    * Rusts! One of the best looking UI mods this game has. Makes the Bills UI far easier to work with
* [Nice Health Tab](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3328729902)
    * Storms! Another one from Andromeda. Does the same for the Health tab
* [Gotta Go Fast](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3712112476)
    * Speeds up pathfinding for pawns that move faster than normal, or that fly
* [BetterWeight](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2221387317)
    * Gives buildings real weights based on what they are built from. Steel Pushing and Iron Pulling on Scadrial
      feel right with it installed. Heavy steel walls resist a Coinshot's Push. Light wooden furniture goes flying
